# Explainer slide review checklist

Run this on the **rendered image**, card by card, pretending you are a junior engineer who has never heard of the work. Every "no" is a fix before showing the slide.

## Whole slide
- [ ] Headline states a surprising fact + the action, and its numbers match the fact sheet.
- [ ] 3–6 numbered cards, in story order, each with its own accent colour.
- [ ] No two cards say the same thing.
- [ ] Every number on the slide appears in the fact sheet with a source; no mismatch with any companion text.
- [ ] One word per concept everywhere; no undefined nickname, abbreviation or notation.
- [ ] Colour meanings are consistent across cards; every colour/shade/symbol has a legend where it appears.
- [ ] Conclusion banner (with size and limits) and lesson banner are present.
- [ ] No overlapping or clipped text; nothing outside its box; no large empty area; readable on a phone.

## Each card
- [ ] Title is a question; one bold takeaway sentence answers it.
- [ ] Data label says which data and why that data.
- [ ] One main figure; the footnote holds caveats only, no reasoning.

## Problem card
- [ ] The problem is measured, not asserted.
- [ ] Targets are named, and anything deliberately not targeted is explained.
- [ ] Each problem is paired with its fix (by name used later on the slide).

## Method card
- [ ] 3–5 numbered cells, each: caption (WHAT) → real-data picture → visible WHY box.
- [ ] Each caption says concretely what is compared with what.
- [ ] The "why this choice" and "what it does NOT affect, and why" questions are answered inside the cells.
- [ ] A before → after picture shows the mechanism working.
- [ ] Worked example labelled "Example:" and the general rule stated.
- [ ] Any simplification in the pictures is admitted.

## Counts card
- [ ] Each count shows its chain (a → b → c) and totals visibly add up; leftovers explained.
- [ ] Selection rules and thresholds are stated.
- [ ] Different data sources are visually distinguished, with a legend.

## Result card
- [ ] Tested on data never used to build the method (and a second check if available).
- [ ] Gains and losses both shown.
- [ ] Statistics in plain words.
- [ ] Per-item change chart + an explanation for every change, including losses and side effects.
- [ ] Claims match what the chart shows.
